Your mic in every clip.

## Everyone

- **Mic and game audio together:** with the mic on, clips start with game and mic mixed into one track, so you hear both in any player, in Discord and in Reruns. Before, the mic was recorded on a second track that players skip, so it seemed to be missing.
- The editor still sets the game and mic levels separately for an edit.
- Clips saved before this update keep the mic on its own track. To hear it, open the clip in Reruns' editor and render it, or pick the second audio track in a player like VLC (Audio → Audio Track).

Bugs: [open an issue](https://github.com/lpears0n/reruns/issues) with your log (Settings → General → Troubleshooting → Logs).
